Second owner-look round on bbk-icl.1: all 105 stories shot, owner signed off on
the batch. Two story defects fixed in 56ead80, three judgement calls filed
rather than decided — bbk-42y, bbk-7jw, bbk-pqd.

The thing I'd pick up first is **bbk-42y**. Clash light's highlight-on-card
measures 1.170 where forest light/dark and clash dark all sit near 1.7, so a
hovered row in that one combination barely moves. It clears contrast.ts's 1.15
floor by 0.02, which is the real finding: the floor is low enough to bless a
fill nobody can see. I'd want to raise the highlight floor toward ~1.4 *and*
retune clash light, not just retune — but the palette value is the owner's
taste, so it needs their eye before I touch it.

What still nags: the four-up width bug had been sitting in plain sight through
every previous look round. The grid-of-cards story was losing half its cards in
two of four palettes and nobody caught it, because a screenshot that is cut off
looks like a screenshot of a narrower thing. The trap
`four-up-budget-is-608px` now has the exact number, but the general lesson is
that a capture can't be trusted to show its own truncation. I checked the other
stories with a pixel scan of the rightmost column rather than by looking, and
that's what found it clean. Worth doing that scan in shoot.mjs itself — warn
when the last column isn't uniform background — but I didn't, and it's not
filed. It would have caught this months ago.

Also rejected but still tempting: I nearly "fixed" the mid-word tag break in
bbk-7jw by adding a min-width to Badge. Don't — `wrap-anywhere` is load-bearing
(see the flex-controls-need-wrap-anywhere trap) and a min-width would quietly
reintroduce the overflow it exists to prevent. If bbk-7jw gets picked up, the
floor belongs on the card, not the control.
