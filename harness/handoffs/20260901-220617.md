# handoff 2026-09-01

Shipped Surface (bbk-rar.4, commit 0db35d3) and closed it. Reviews were clean
except one real gap: nothing in the kit warned on an unknown prop value, which
the plan asks for at line 81. Built it as src/lib/classes.ts rather than an
inline check in Surface — Text and Stack still need routing through it, bbk-3cy.

The decision I was least sure of is Surface owning its padding (p-4). It makes a
bare Surface look right everywhere, but it means CardMedia will have to bleed
with a negative margin when Card arrives, and negative margins are the kind of
thing that looks like a workaround to whoever reads it next. The alternative —
no padding on Surface, all of it on the Card parts — is cleaner for Card and
worse for every other caller. I still think padding is right, but look at it
again the moment CardMedia is real; if the bleed is ugly, that is the signal.

Nested surfaces share a radius (both rounded-lg), so an inner box sits with its
corners exactly concentric with the outer. It reads fine in the screenshots and
I left it. A tighter inner radius is the textbook answer and would need a token.

Next: bbk-rar.5 — Skeleton, Separator and EmptyState. Skeleton is the first
component with motion, so the reduced-motion media query gets its first outing.
