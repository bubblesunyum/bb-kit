Two things I was still turning over:

The `Styled` helper in text.tsx narrows with `element as "p"` and a second cast on the
spread. It is sound at runtime — React spreads whatever it gets onto whatever tag it is
handed — and correctness confirmed that, but it is two casts in a component this small.
I tried four typings before it: an intersection of p-props and label-props (reduces to
never, because every event handler is element-typed and contravariant), a shared
`Ref<HTMLElement>`, an index signature, and a plain `ElementType`. The tempting fifth is
to drop `Styled` entirely and let each of the seven wrappers write its own JSX against a
shared `textClass()` helper — fully typed, no casts, at the cost of seven near-identical
three-line bodies. If VStack/HStack or Surface hit the same wall, that is probably the
shape to move to for all of them at once.

`wrap-break-word` is on every Text by default. It is right for a pasted URL in a
paragraph and I am less sure it is right for a Label or a Span inside a table cell,
where breaking mid-word may be worse than a scrollbar. Nothing has surfaced that yet —
worth watching once ListItem exists and text lives inside a constrained box.
