The thing I couldn't pin down: shoot.mjs's new selection rule refuses to claim
sibling stories for a `.tsx` with no story of its own, which is right for
src/components (16 siblings would bury the real capture) and wrong in spirit
for something like theme-provider.tsx, which genuinely does affect all 16. The
`.tsx` test is a proxy for "is this a component", and it will be wrong the first
time a non-component .tsx lands beside stories. I left it because the honest
alternative — a per-directory declaration of what a shared source is — is more
machinery than the problem has yet earned. Worth revisiting if it bites.

Also new: a trap worth recalling before touching the palette —
`bd recall new-role-needs-a-color-line-in-globals`. Adding a role to palette.ts
does nothing until globals.css gets a matching `--color-<role>` line, and the
failure surfaces as an axe contrast violation naming the *page* colour, which
reads like a bad colour rather than a missing mapping. It cost a full --full
run to diagnose.
